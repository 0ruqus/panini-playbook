How every sandwich is built

The house formula

01 · Bread

Structure

Has to survive the press. Sourdough, Texas toast, ciabatta, focaccia, rye.

02 · Melt

Glue

Cheese on the top and bottom slices. It holds the fillings and lets you open the sandwich after cooking.

03 · Fill

Main event

Turkey, ham, roast beef, chicken, vegetables, fruit.

04 · Spread

Signature

Sweet, spicy or tangy. Jams, aiolis, pesto, mustards, glazes.

05 · Add

After the press

Wet, fresh items go in after cooking: avocado, tomato, lettuce, kimchi.

06 · Fry spread

Outside

Mayonnaise by default, often with garlic. Butter for desserts.

Default fry spread: unless a recipe says otherwise, spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on the outside of both slices. It browns more evenly than butter and doesn't burn as quickly. Stir in minced or roasted garlic for garlic-toasted bread.

At the press

Press technique

Wet ingredients go in after the press

Avocado, tomato, lettuce, kimchi and other wet ingredients turn soggy and steam the bread if you press them. Add them after cooking.

  1. Put cheese right against the bread on both the top and bottom slices, with the hot fillings in between.
  2. Press until the cheese melts.
  3. Lift the top slice, opening just below its layer of melted cheese so the gooey cheese stays stuck to the bread.
  4. Add the wet ingredients on top of the hot fillings, close it and cut.

Heat and time

375°Fabout 3 min · thin melts
200°Fabout 10 min · thick, meaty

Lift the lid and check now and then. When you hear cheese sizzling on the grates, it's melted.

Cook dessert paninis last. They leak hard, so press them after the savory ones, and use butter outside instead of mayo.

Bread

  • Focaccia and hearty whole-grain breads hold up to a hot press.
  • Sourdough, rye and French bread all handle high heat.
  • Pita and biscuits burn easily, so use low heat.
  • Thinner or lighter sandwiches also like a lower setting.
  • Keep a spray bottle of water nearby. If the bread is getting too hard or dry, give the outside a quick mist.

Cheese

  • 4 clumps of brie ≈ 2 oz.
  • Cream cheese is a great filler with a strong cheese like Swiss. It softens the bite and adds creaminess.
  • Laughing Cow wedges (plain, garlic or hot) add cheap creaminess next to a sliced cheese.
  • Cheese on both slices locks the fillings in.

Chipotle Aioli

Recipe
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 cup oil
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 can chipotles in adobo, to taste
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • 2–4 cloves roasted garlic
  • 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  1. Blend the yolks and lime juice for about 30 seconds.
  2. With the blender running, add the oil in a thin, steady stream over 1–2 minutes. Stop at the thickness you like.
  3. Add the garlic, chipotle, seasonings, cilantro, vinegar and maple. Blend briefly.
  4. Let it rest so the flavors blend.

Shortcut: stir adobo sauce, a minced chipotle and lime into store-bought mayo.

Pineapple Habanero Salsa

Recipe
  • 6 firm Roma tomatoes
  • 2 cups pineapple (250 g), grilled if you like
  • 3–5 cloves garlic
  • 2 serranos (30 g)
  • 4 jalapeños (125 g)
  • 4 habaneros (24 g)
  • ½ jumbo red onion (200 g)
  • A handful of cilantro
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon and 2 limes
  1. Air-fry the serranos, jalapeños and habaneros at 450°F. Watch closely and roast lightly.
  2. Roughly cut the onion and peppers, and mince the garlic.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and pineapple. Save the pineapple juice and mix it with the citrus juice and zest.
  4. Combine everything and pulse in a food processor.

This is the base for the roasted pineapple habanero sauce: blend smoother and reduce slightly. It comes out medium. For real heat, use 7–8 habaneros.

Blueberry Balsamic Reduction

Recipe
  • ½ cup blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1½ Tbsp brown sugar
  1. Simmer over medium-high heat until syrupy.
  2. Strain out the berries.
  3. Spread on buttered bread, then layer cheese and spinach.

Lemon Curd

Recipe
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 Tbsp lemon zest
  • 6 egg yolks
  • About ½ cup lemon juice
  • ½ cup cold butter, cubed
  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar, then add the lemon juice.
  2. Cook in a rice cooker on sauté or simmer, whisking constantly, until it thickens and coats a spoon.
  3. Off the heat, whisk in the butter. Strain for a silky finish.

Based on Preppy Kitchen's lemon curd (preppykitchen.com/lemon-curd). It keeps about two weeks in the fridge.

PBR Beer-Caramelized Onions

Recipe · about 1 hr
  • 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)
  • 1 can Pabst Blue Ribbon (12 oz)
  1. Melt the butter with the oil over medium-low heat. Add the onions and salt.
  2. Cook slowly, stirring now and then, until soft and golden, about 30 minutes.
  3. Add PBR a splash at a time, letting each splash cook off and scraping up the brown bits.
  4. Keep going for another 20–30 minutes, until the onions are deep brown and jammy.

The house version uses no sugar. For sweeter, darker onions, stir in the brown sugar before the first splash of beer. Use it on the Fig & Caramelized Onion panini, or anywhere a card calls for caramelized onions: French Onion, Club Modified, Buffalo Apple Chicken. It keeps about a week in the fridge.

Chimichurri

Recipe
  • 1 cup packed flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp fresh oregano (or 2 tsp dried)
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt and pepper
  1. Stir the garlic, vinegar, salt and pepper flakes together and let them sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the parsley and oregano, then the olive oil.
  3. Rest at least 20 minutes before using.

A standard version, not from the notes. Great on roast beef or Italian beef. It's wet, so add it after the press.

Crock Pot Italian Beef

Recipe
  • 3 lb rump roast
  • 1 pkg Good Seasons Italian dressing mix
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 pkg au jus gravy mix
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 6 pepperoncini peppers
  1. Mix everything and pour it over the roast in a 5-quart crock pot.
  2. Cook on low for 4 hours.
  3. Take out the roast, slice it thin, return it to the pot and cook on low 1 more hour.

Serve on toasted hoagies with mozzarella or provolone and giardiniera.

Giardiniera

Recipe · 3 days
  • ½ head cauliflower, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 4 serranos, sliced
  • ½ onion, chopped
  • ¾ cup white vinegar, plus more to cover
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 Tbsp crushed red pepper
  • ½ Tbsp dried parsley
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ¾ tsp ground pepper
  • ½ tsp each celery seed, mustard seed, fennel seed, coriander seed, dill weed
  • ¼ tsp ground allspice

Combine everything, adding vinegar until the vegetables are submerged. Cover and refrigerate at least 3 days.
