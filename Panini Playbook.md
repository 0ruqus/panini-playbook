## The house formula

Every sandwich here is built in the same six layers:

| Layer | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Bread** | The structure. It has to survive the press | Sourdough, Texas toast, ciabatta, focaccia, marble rye, French roll |
| **Melt** | The "glue": cheese on the top *and* bottom slices. It holds the fillings and lets you open the sandwich after cooking | Havarti, Swiss, provolone, brie, smoked gouda, pepper jack |
| **Fill** | The main event | Turkey, ham, roast beef, chicken, vegetables, fruit |
| **Spread** | The flavor signature, usually sweet, spicy or tangy | Jams, aiolis, pesto, mustards, glazes |
| **Add** | Wet and fresh items, added **after** pressing | Avocado, tomato, lettuce, kimchi, banana peppers, arugula |
| **Fry spread** | What goes on the *outside* of the bread | **Mayonnaise** (default), garlic mayo, butter |

> **Default fry spread:** unless a recipe says otherwise, spread a thin layer of **mayonnaise on the outside of both slices** before pressing. It browns more evenly than butter and won't burn as quickly. Stir in minced or roasted garlic for garlic-toasted bread. Dessert paninis use butter instead.

## Press technique

**Wet ingredients go in after the press**
Avocado, tomato, lettuce, kimchi and other wet ingredients turn soggy and steam the bread if you press them. Add them after cooking:
1. Put cheese right against the bread on **both** the top and bottom slices, with the hot fillings in between.
2. Press until the cheese melts.
3. Lift the top slice, opening just **below its layer of melted cheese** so the gooey cheese stays stuck to the bread.
4. Add the wet ingredients on top of the hot fillings, close it and cut.

This applies to every recipe below. Anything in the **Add** line, and any tomato, lettuce or avocado in the Fill line, goes in after pressing.

**Heat and time**
- **Thin sandwiches** (grilled cheese, simple melts) only need the cheese melted and the bread toasted. Press at **medium-high, about 375°F, for about 3 minutes**.
- **Thick sandwiches with meat to heat through** do better **low and slow, about 200°F, for about 10 minutes**.
- Lift the lid and check now and then. **When you hear cheese sizzling on the grates, it's melted.**

**Bread**
- **Focaccia** holds up very well under a hot press, and so do most hearty or whole-grain breads.
- **Sourdough, rye and French bread** all handle high heat.
- **Pita and biscuits** burn easily, so use a lower heat setting.
- A **lower heat** also suits thinner sandwiches or ones with less meat.
- **Keep a spray bottle of water nearby.** If the bread is getting too hard or dry, give the outside a quick mist.

**Cheese**
- Brie portions: **4 clumps of brie ≈ 2 oz**.
- **Cream cheese** is a great filler with a strong cheese like Swiss. It softens the bite and adds creaminess.
- Laughing Cow spreadable wedges (plain, garlic or hot) are a cheap way to add creaminess. Use them alongside a sliced cheese.
- Put cheese on **both** the top and bottom slices (for example, mozzarella top and bottom) so the fillings are locked in.

**Dessert paninis go last.** They leak hard, so press them after the savory ones, and use **butter** as the fry coating instead of mayo.

## House spreads & components

### Chipotle Aioli
- 3 large egg yolks
- 1 cup oil
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt and pepper
- 1 can whole chipotle chiles in adobo (use to taste)
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- 2–4 cloves roasted garlic (raw works too)
- 2 Tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 tsp white vinegar
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika

1. Blend the yolks and lime juice for about 30 seconds.
2. With the blender running, add the oil in a thin, steady stream over 1–2 minutes. Stop when it reaches the thickness you like.
3. Add the garlic, chipotle, seasonings, cilantro, vinegar and maple syrup. Blend briefly.
4. Let it rest so the flavors blend. A short rest is enough, but longer is better.

*Shortcut:* stir adobo sauce, a minced chipotle and lime into store-bought mayo.

### Pineapple Habanero Salsa
This is the base for the roasted pineapple habanero sauce. Blend it smoother and reduce it slightly to make a spread.

- 6 firm Roma tomatoes
- 2 cups pineapple (about 250 g), grilled if you like
- 3–5 cloves garlic
- 2 serranos (about 30 g)
- 4 jalapeños (about 125 g)
- 4 habaneros (about 24 g)
- ½ jumbo red onion (about 200 g)
- A handful of cilantro
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon and 2 limes

1. Air-fry the serranos, jalapeños and habaneros at 450°F. Watch them closely and roast them lightly.
2. Roughly cut the onion and peppers, and mince the garlic.
3. Dice the tomatoes and pineapple. Save the pineapple juice and mix it with the citrus juice and zest.
4. Combine everything and pulse in a food processor to spread the heat evenly.

*Heat note:* it comes out medium. For real heat, use 7–8 habaneros.

### Blueberry Balsamic Reduction
- ½ cup fresh or frozen blueberries
- 1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1½ Tbsp brown sugar

Simmer everything over medium-high heat until syrupy, then strain out the berries. Spread it on buttered bread, then layer the cheese and spinach.

### Lemon Curd
This uses Preppy Kitchen's lemon curd recipe (preppykitchen.com/lemon-curd), made in a rice cooker:
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp lemon zest
- 6 egg yolks
- About ½ cup lemon juice
- ½ cup cold butter

Whisk the yolks and sugar, then add the lemon juice. Cook on sauté or simmer, whisking constantly, until it thickens and coats a spoon. Take it off the heat, then whisk in the cubed butter. Strain if you want it silky. It keeps about two weeks in the fridge.

### Cowboy Caviar
- 1 can black-eyed peas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup chopped tomato
- ¾ cup corn kernels
- 1 cup chopped avocado
- ½ cup red onion
- ¼ cup cilantro
- Dressing: 4 Tbsp olive oil, 2 Tbsp lime juice, 1 grated garlic clove, 2 tsp fine sea salt, 1 tsp black pepper

Whisk the dressing and toss it with everything else. Add the avocado last.

### Bulgogi Marinade
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp light brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 2 Tbsp rice wine (or red wine or mirin)
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 2 Tbsp minced garlic
- 1 Tbsp cider vinegar
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 Tbsp chopped green onion
- 2 Tbsp pear purée (or 1 Tbsp kiwi or 3 Tbsp onion purée)
- ½ yellow onion, slivered

Marinate thin-sliced beef, then sear it hot before building the sandwich.

### PBR Beer-Caramelized Onions
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 can (12 oz) Pabst Blue Ribbon

1. Melt the butter with the oil over medium-low heat. Add the onions and salt.
2. Cook slowly, stirring now and then, until soft and golden, about 30 minutes.
3. Stir in the brown sugar. Add PBR a splash at a time, letting each splash cook off and scraping up the brown bits.
4. Keep going for another 20–30 minutes, until the onions are deep brown and jammy.

Use it on the Fig & Caramelized Onion panini, or anywhere a card calls for caramelized onions: French Onion, Club Modified, Buffalo Apple Chicken. It keeps about a week in the fridge.

### Chimichurri
*A standard version, not from the notes.*
- 1 cup packed flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 2 Tbsp fresh oregano (or 2 tsp dried)
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- ½ cup olive oil
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper

1. Stir the garlic, vinegar, salt and pepper flakes together and let them sit for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the parsley and oregano, then the olive oil.
3. Rest at least 20 minutes before using.

Great on roast beef or Italian beef. It's wet, so add it after the press.

### Crock Pot Italian Beef
- 3 lb rump roast
- 1 pkg Good Seasons Italian dressing mix
- 1 cup water
- 1 pkg au jus gravy mix
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 6 pepperoncini peppers

1. Mix everything and pour it over the roast in a 5-quart crock pot.
2. Cook on low for 4 hours.
3. Take out the roast, slice it thin, return it to the pot and cook on low 1 more hour.

Serve on toasted hoagies with mozzarella or provolone and giardiniera.

### Giardiniera
- **Vegetables:** ½ head cauliflower (chopped), 1 red bell pepper (diced), 2 carrots (diced), 4 serranos (sliced), ½ onion (chopped)
- **Spices:** 1 Tbsp crushed red pepper, ½ Tbsp dried parsley, 1 tsp dried oregano, ¾ tsp ground pepper, ½ tsp each of celery seed, mustard seed, fennel seed, coriander seed and dill weed, ¼ tsp ground allspice
- **Liquids:** ¾ cup white vinegar (plus more to cover), ½ cup vegetable oil

Combine everything, adding vinegar until the vegetables are submerged. Cover and refrigerate at least 3 days.
